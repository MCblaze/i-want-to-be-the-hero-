# Main scene visual and gameplay repair — 16 September 2026

Baseline: a3b4ec5. This is an in-progress production pass, not release qualification.

## Reproduced in live Unity

- Ground art was below the collision surface, making Logan and enemies visibly float.
- Full-width terrain illustrations were squeezed onto short ledges, making their stone detail far smaller than the ground.
- Six background cards exposed rectangular edges; their fixed world positions and height did not cover the camera view.
- Twenty-one placeholder scenery renderers still drew translucent rectangles over production backgrounds.
- Main had no HeroTuning connection. Double jump, wand swapping/firing and backflip were conditional on the movement lab. Earlier statements that these were playable in Main overstated completion.

## Repairs

- Sixteen static surface skins now use sliced sprites with their walk-line pivots aligned to collider tops; ten ledges use a separate detail crop. Original source PNGs remain unchanged.
- The teal lift uses a walk-line sprite and aligns to its collider top.
- Placeholder backdrop/trunk/canopy/water renderers are disabled while their editable objects remain.
- Zone cards fill the camera view with overscan and subtle drift, maintain an opaque base, and crossfade in world-route order. The background is toned down for actor/terrain separation. Sibling references are cached.
- MainSceneLayout references the existing MovementTuning asset; controller abilities now read it in Main as well as the lab. Main's existing Spark sword damage is preserved.
- Five hazard placeholders now use a transparent thorn cluster with the existing hazard bounds and no added colliders.
- Main camera limits now account for the actual viewport width, preventing the old widescreen clamp from hiding Logan in narrow windows.
- Live Canopy review exposed large opaque particle squares. The water-mote system had no material and used size 1. It now uses a transparent soft-mote shader, 0.035–0.075-unit particles, a 24-particle cap and lifetime fade. The shader compiled without errors and live capture confirmed the squares disappeared. Reduced-effects mode clears/stops the particles.

## Generated hazard provenance

`Assets/Art/Sunleaf/Terrain/ThornHazard.png` is original AI-generated artwork from this production pass. Direction: side-on Sunleaf mossy stone and brown-purple thorn cluster, ivory tips, pixel-art treatment, no perspective top plane, true transparent background. The first candidate had a baked checkerboard and was rejected. The second was a transparency edit, saved immediately to local staging and Unity; PNG color type 6 confirms RGBA. Native Sprite cropping aligns the visible hazard to its existing collision bounds. Source PNG is retained unchanged. Final visual/client acceptance remains open.
